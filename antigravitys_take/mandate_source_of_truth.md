## 3. The V-Model Methodology: Top-Down Design & Bottom-Up Testing

Architectural integrity requires a dual-directional development philosophy. We balance **top-down specification** (defining requirements) with **bottom-up validation** (ensuring atomic readiness).

### Top-Down System Design
 
The design phase mandates a flow from high-level abstraction to granular execution:
 
1. **System Requirements**: Define business goals and constraints.
2. **Broad Strokes**: Outline primary architecture and agent workflows.
3. **Subsystems**: Deconstruct into functional components (e.g., UI vs. Booking Engine).
4. **Sub-subsystems**: Design the individual, atomic units of work within each subsystem to be executed by specific agents.

### Bottom-Up Testing: The Failure Containment Loop
 
Testing begins at the smallest possible level—the sub-subsystem—to prevent cascading failures.
 
* **Micro-Level Redesign**: If a unit fails verification, it must be redesigned immediately.

> [!NOTE]
> **So What?** Redesigning at the atomic level is a strategic safeguard. It is exponentially cheaper to fix a faulty logic gate than to debug a failed multi-agent integration during launch.
