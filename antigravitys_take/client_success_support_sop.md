# Client Success & Support: Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

## 1. The Interaction Philosophy
Crystal Shadows' support is an extension of the reading itself: **Calm, Punctual, and Professional.**

## 2. Workflow: New Client Engagement
*   **Instant Confirmation**: Personalized automated email with the "Preparation Guide" for their reading (Birth time, Quiet environment, etc.).
*   **24-Hour Reminder**: SMS notification to prevent no-shows.
*   **Follow-up**: 48 hours post-reading, an automated "Integration Reflection" email is sent, inviting the client to reflect on the wisdom shared.

## 3. Conflict Resolution & Refunds
To maintain the "Masterpiece" reputation, we implement a clear, friction-less policy:
*   **Rescheduling**: Allowed up to 12 hours prior with zero fee.
*   **Refunds**: Provided if Crystal cannot attend; 50% retained if the client is a "No-Show" without notification, protecting her 30-year legacy time value.
*   **Resolution Tone**: "We understand life shifts like the stars; let us find a more aligned time for your wisdom."

## 4. Feedback Loop
*   **The Wisdom Survey**: A 3-question survey (Wait time, Clarity, Overall Impact) used to continuously refine the "Sage" persona.
