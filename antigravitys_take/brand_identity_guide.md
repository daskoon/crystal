# Brand Identity Manual & Voice Guide

## 1. The Brand Persona: The Sage Master
Crystal Shadows is not an "entertainer." She is a **Master Practitioner**.
*   **Core Promise**: 30 years of clarity for life's most complex shifts.
*   **Visual Persona**: Deep, cosmic, elegant, and grounded.

## 2. Voice & Tone Guidelines
| Category | Tone | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Greeting** | Warm but Authoritative | "Welcome. Let us look at what the stars have mapped for your next decade." |
| **Crisis** | Calm & Compassionate | "The current transit suggests a period of internal pruning. It is not an end, but a preparation." |
| **Marketing** | Logic-Backed | "30 years of observation meets timeless wisdom. Your path, illuminated." |

### Forbidden Language (Blacklist)
*   "Fortune telling" (implies guesswork).
*   "Magic" (implies performance).
*   "Spooky/Mystical vibes" (implies trendiness over substance).

## 3. Visual Grammar
*   **Imagery**: High-contrast textures (Ice, Deep Water, Nebula, Ancient Parchment).
*   **Spacing**: The "1.8x Grid" — generous spacing between elements to reflect "Deep Breath" and "Clarity."
*   **Logo Treatment**: Gold-leaf foil effects against deep purple gradients.
