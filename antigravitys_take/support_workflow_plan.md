# Customer Support & Workflow Plan

## 1. Interaction Channels
*   **Primary**: Direct phone for "Old-School/High-Touch" clients.
*   **Secondary**: SMS/Email for "Tech-Native" clients.
*   **Automated**: Instant booking confirmation via Square/Acuity.

## 2. Tone of Support
*   **Principles**: Compassion, Punctuality, and Clarity.
*   **Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)**: All inquiries responded to within 4 business hours to maintain "High-Authority" presence.

## 3. Conflict Resolution
*   **Policy**: Clear refund guidelines in the booking footer to eliminate the "billing friction" identified in research audits.
*   **Escalation**: Direct path to Crystal for complex scheduling or deep-reading adjustments.
