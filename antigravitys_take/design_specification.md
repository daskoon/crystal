# System Requirements & Broad Strokes: Crystal Shadows Relaunch

## 1. High-Level Requirements
 
* **Business Goal**: Re-establish Crystal Shadows as a high-authority, 30-year legacy brand with a modern, friction-less conversion funnel.
* **Target Audience**: High-intent seekers of astrology, tarot, and spiritual guidance who value experience and privacy.
* **Functional Constraints**:
  * Direct-to-Call/Text lead generation.
  * Automated booking system integration (Square/Acuity).
  * No informal "social media" photography; strictly professional/atmospheric assets.
  * Legal disclaimer for "Entertainment Purposes Only" (PA Law Compliance).

## 2. Broad Strokes Architecture
 
* **Tech Stack**: Modern Vanilla HTML5/CSS3 (No structural rot).
* **Design Philosophy**: "Shadow & Light" - A high-contrast, mystical aesthetic using a deep purple and matte teal palette.
* **Navigation Pattern**: Single-page high-conversion layout with sticky lead-gen contact points.

## 3. Subsystem Breakdown
 
1. **Identity Layer**: Typography (Yellowtail), Color tokens, and Logo assets.
2. **Conversion Engine**: Call/Text buttons, Booking Modals, and Rate information.
3. **Authority Layer**: Google Review integration, "Legendary Predictions" section (Heritage), and Professional Gallery.
4. **Compliance Layer**: Global Footer with Legal and Social links.

## 4. Sub-subsystem Micro-Design (Atomic Units)
 
* **Unit 4.1**: Sticky Call Button (Mobile optimized).
* **Unit 4.2**: Heritage Timeline Component (Restoring news clips).
* **Unit 4.3**: Dynamic Pricing Grid (Syncing with live rates).
