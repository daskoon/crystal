# Brand Identity & Voice Manual

## 1. The Brand Persona
*   **Identity**: The Master Practitioner.
*   **Attributes**: Wise, Professional, Empathetic, Authoritative, Clear.
*   **Role**: A guide, not a performer.

## 2. Voice & Tone
*   **DO**: Use "Wisdom," "Legacy," "Clarity," "Illumination."
*   **DON'T**: Use "Magic Tricks," "Fortune Telling," "Spooky," "Vague."
*   **Tone**: Calm, high-fidelity, and grounded in 30 years of observation.

## 3. Visual Grammar
*   **Imagery**: Celestial, cosmic, abstract light, high-quality textures.
*   **Spacing**: Generous white (or dark) space to allow the "Spiritual Breath" of the site.
