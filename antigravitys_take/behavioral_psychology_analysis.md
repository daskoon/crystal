# Behavioral Psychology & Nudge Analysis

## 1. Trust Architecture (The "Sage" Nudge)
The "Masterpiece" UI leverages **Authority Bias** and **Social Proof** to justify premium pricing.

*   **Visual Authority**: High-contrast glassmorphism signals "Modern Integrity."
*   **The Heritage Nudge**: Placing the "30 Years" badge adjacent to the booking CTA serves as a "Reassurance Anchor" at the exact moment of financial commitment.

## 2. Cognitive Heuristics
*   **The Halo Effect**: By leading with Phil’s Prediction and Media logos, we transfer the credibility of established institutions to Crystal’s individual sessions.
*   **Friction Reduction**: Using a 3-step booking flow (Select -> Time -> Pay) prevents "Choice Overload" and reduces drop-off.

## 3. Scarcity & Urgency (Ethical Implementation)
*   **The Waitlist Nudge**: Displaying "Next Available" rather than "Book Now" shifts the perception from a commodity to a sought-after exclusive session.
*   **Priority Access**: Offering a small premium for "Emergency Readings" within 24 hours.

## 4. Paradox of Choice
By limiting the primary homepage to three distinct paths (Astrology, Tarot, Crystals), we ensure the user feels guided rather than overwhelmed by the depth of Crystal's wisdom.
