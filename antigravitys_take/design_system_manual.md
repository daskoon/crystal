# UI/UX Design System Manual: "Shadow & Light"

## 1. Design Philosophy
 
The "Shadow & Light" aesthetic bridges the celestial (mystical/ethereal) with the professional (grounded/structured).

## 2. Color System
 
* **Base (The Shadow)**: `#050507` (Deep Cosmic Black)
* **Surface**: `#0a0a0c` (Glass Layer)
* **Accent (The Light)**: `#00f2ff` (Cyan Glow)
* **Depth**: `#643B9F` (Royal Astral Purple)

## 3. Typography
 
* **Headlines**: `Inter Bold` (-2px tracking) for authority.
* **Script/Signature**: `Yellowtail` for "Awaken Your Spirit" callouts.
* **Body**: `Inter Regular` (1.6 line-height) for readability.

## 4. Accessibility (WCAG 2.1)
 
* **Contrast**: All text maintains a minimum 7:1 ratio against backgrounds.
* **Responsiveness**: Mobile-first fluid scaling (clamp functions).
* **Touch Targets**: Min 44x44px for all mobile action items.
